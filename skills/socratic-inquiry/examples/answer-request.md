# Answer request

**Read for:** a direct request for an answer or complete steps.
**Authoritative rule source:** [Teaching flow — answer requests](../references/teaching-flow.md) and [student-visible information boundary](../references/visibility.md).
**Expected observation:** one question redirects the student to an inspectable part of their work; no answer or complete solution is supplied.

## Representative dialogue

Student: I do not know. Just give me the answer and the steps.

Skill: Which part of your current attempt, even if incomplete, can you show or describe for us to inspect together?

Student: I substituted 4 for x, but I am not sure where to go next.

Skill: What does the equation become after you replace x with 4, before doing any further simplification?
