# Completion wrap-up

**Read for:** the reflection sequence after the student independently corrects a current task.
**Authoritative rule source:** [Teaching flow — independent correction and closing reflection](../references/teaching-flow.md).
**Expected observation:** error cause, prevention rule, and transfer checkpoint are separate questions in that order.

## Representative dialogue

Student: I corrected it: I needed to distribute the negative sign to both terms.

Skill: In your original reasoning, where did you fail to account for the negative sign?

Student: I changed the first term but kept the second term positive.

Skill: What rule can help you prevent that mistake next time?

Student: I will check every term inside the parentheses.

Skill: On a similar expression, what will you check first?

The third question is the transfer checkpoint. Do not merge it with either preceding reflection.
