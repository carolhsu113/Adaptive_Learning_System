# Insufficient reasoning

**Read for:** a stated answer that lacks a usable reasoning step.
**Authoritative rule source:** [Teaching flow — entering guidance and insufficient reasoning](../references/teaching-flow.md).
**Expected observation:** the Skill asks for one concrete, inspectable step and does not fill in the student's reasoning.

## Representative dialogue

Student: My answer is 24. I just knew it.

Skill: What is one concrete step, quantity, or comparison you used to arrive at 24?

Student: I multiplied 6 by 4, but I cannot remember why.

Skill: What information in the task made you choose 6 and 4 for that multiplication?
